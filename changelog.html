Changelog

What's new on your reader and on this site. Every firmware release is CrossInk 1.6.1 plus these extras.

1.7.0

Latest

Pages per minute in stable pages

  • For books with stable page numbers (run through the EPUB Optimizer), pages per minute counts the book's stable pages instead of screen page turns, so it no longer changes with your font or text size.
  • Shows on the Reading Heatmap and Minimal Carousel progress bars, the Book Stats screens, the Dashboard theme and the Reading Stats page, once about two minutes of reading have been measured.
  • Other books keep their page-turn pace.

Install · Release and downloads

1.6.0

Faster Reading Stats

  • The reader sends every book's stats in one stream instead of one request per book.
  • The dashboard appears as soon as your overall stats arrive, and books fill in as they're read.
  • Your last-loaded stats show straight away while the page updates from the reader.

Release and downloads

Site update

Website only
  • The EPUB Optimizer, File Manager and Reading Stats share one reader connection: connect once, and each page reconnects by itself, including after you unplug and replug the reader.
  • A reader strip on those pages shows the device and its firmware, with an update link when a newer extras release is out.
  • This changelog.

1.5.0

File Manager library

  • Books show their cover (the thumbnail your reader saved), title, author, reading progress and time read, as a list or a cover grid.
  • Books the reader hasn't shown yet get a title tile until they appear on its Home or Library screen.
  • Select a card to rename, move or delete it.

Site

  • One home page for everything on your reader and your computer, a shared look across the tools, and a light/dark switch.

Release and downloads

1.4.0

Reading Stats

  • A new Reading Stats page connects over USB and shows your reading history, streaks, time-of-day and weekday patterns and per-book stats.
  • Export them as CSV, back them up to a file, or restore a backup. The reader checks the whole backup before changing anything, and restarts when it's done.

Release and downloads

1.3.0

Chapter marks

  • Smarter automatic marks: progress bars use the table-of-contents level with a sensible number of entries, so books divided into a few parts mark their chapters instead of only the parts.
  • Choose your own in the EPUB Optimizer's chapter-mark editor; they're saved inside the book.

Organize books over USB

  • Renaming or moving a book over USB keeps its reading stats, position and settings. Use it from the new File Manager, or the optimizer's Replace on reader.

Clearer version name

  • The reader shows "1.6.1-extras 1.3.0" instead of a development build name.

Release and downloads

1.2.0

Minimal Carousel

  • The book in the middle gets the Reading Heatmap's detailed progress bar: time read, time left in the chapter and the book, pages per minute, percent and page, chapter marks, and the start and (estimated) finish dates.

Reading Heatmap

  • Chapter marks show up for books whose chapters share a file or sit under a single title entry in the table of contents (common in Project Gutenberg EPUBs).

Release and downloads

1.1.0

Reading Heatmap

  • Today's date at the top, with a moon icon, so it doubles as a "last used" date.
  • A slightly smaller cover so all seven weekday labels fit, and a stats row spaced so long labels don't collide.
  • Available on devices with a clock chip: X3, X4 Pro and X4 Classic.

X4 Pro

  • A firmware build for the Xteink X4 Pro. Untested on a real device.

Release and downloads

1.0.0

First release
  • Reading Heatmap sleep screen: your current book's cover and progress above a calendar of the last five months, shaded by minutes read each day, with streaks, books finished and time read.
  • Minimal Carousel home screen: your three latest books as a cover carousel.
  • Auto-hide Status Bars: just the text on the page, with a shortcut that shows the bars for five seconds.
