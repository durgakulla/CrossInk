EPUB Optimizer

Make books open fast, show accurate page numbers and look sharp on e-ink.

    • Chapter marks (under each book) set where the progress bars mark chapters; needs firmware v1.3.0+.
    • Send to reader works over USB in Chrome or Edge, with the reader on its Home screen. Replace on reader writes over a book already there, keeping its file name so the reader keeps its reading stats; your place may shift if the chapters were split differently.
    • Copying books another way? After replacing one, use Delete Book Cache in its menu.
    • Start from the original book when you can. DRM-protected books can't be optimized.
    • Uses CrossInk's web-portal optimizer (MIT). Books are optimized in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
