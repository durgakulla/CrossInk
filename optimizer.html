    Good to know

    • Start from the original book when you have it. Optimizing an already-optimized EPUB works, but KOReader progress sync won't map positions for it.
    • Replacing a book on your reader? Upload it through CrossInk's web portal, or use Delete Book Cache in the book's menu after copying it over. Otherwise the reader keeps using the old copy's layout. Your position and stats are kept either way.
    • Protected books (DRM) can't be optimized.
