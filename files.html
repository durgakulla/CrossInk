File Manager

Your reader's library: covers and progress, folders, renames, moves, uploads and downloads.

Connect your reader to see its books and folders.

    Update to CrossInk extras 1.5.0 to see covers, titles and reading progress here.

    • Works over USB in Chrome or Edge, with the reader on its Home screen.
    • Drag items onto a folder or a folder name in the path to move them; drop files from your computer to upload.
    • Moving or renaming a book keeps its reading stats (firmware v1.3.0+). Deleting a book doesn't delete its stats.
    • Uploading a file with the same name as one already there replaces it and keeps that book's stats.
