  • Needs CrossInk extras 1.4.0 or newer, Chrome or Edge, and the reader on its Home screen.
  • A backup is a readable JSON file of all your stats. Restoring replaces the reader's stats with it; books are matched by their path, so keep them in the same folders. The page saves a backup of the current stats first, and the reader restarts when it's done.
